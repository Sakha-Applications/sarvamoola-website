Sarvamoola Foundation

Preserving Tradition. Empowering Communities. Building Futures.

What we do

Sarvamoola Foundation brings together spiritual learning, cultural preservation, education, and community development under a shared vision of service and upliftment. Rooted in traditional values, the Foundation seeks to nurture intellectual and spiritual growth through daily religious discourses, the study of Vedic scriptures and spiritual philosophy, and active community participation.

The Foundation also organizes satsang yatras to sacred and culturally significant destinations across India, with its spiritual journeys extending to Nepal. These gatherings provide opportunities for devotees and families to deepen their spiritual understanding, strengthen community bonds, and experience the richness of India's religious and cultural heritage.

Alongside its spiritual activities, the Foundation is committed to creating opportunities for students, aspiring professionals, and individuals from economically disadvantaged backgrounds. By encouraging skill development, mentorship, and practical exposure to technology, it strives to bridge the gap between traditional knowledge and modern opportunities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to foster a community where spiritual wisdom, education, and opportunity go hand in hand. Through daily religious discourses and community activities, the Foundation encourages individuals of all ages to deepen their understanding of Vedic scriptures, religious philosophy, and traditional spiritual teachings.

An important aspect of this mission is encouraging participation in the annual examinations conducted by Karnataka Samskrit University (KSU). Volunteers and regular attendees of the Foundation's discourses, including men and women across different age groups, actively participate in these examinations covering religious, philosophical, and spiritual subjects. This reflects the Foundation's commitment to making traditional knowledge accessible and encouraging lifelong learning.

Equally important is our commitment to empowering the next generation through technology and professional development. By providing mentorship, practical learning opportunities, and guidance to interns, recent graduates, and aspiring professionals, particularly those from economically disadvantaged backgrounds, we seek to help individuals develop relevant skills, gain confidence, and establish meaningful careers.

Our Founder

Sarvamoola Foundation is guided by the vision of Acharya Bindumadhav Kerur, an alumnus of the prestigious Birla Institute of Technology and Science (BITS Pilani), with over three decades of experience in the technology industry. Having served in diverse roles across multiple organizations throughout his professional career, he brings together extensive industry experience and a deep commitment to spiritual learning, cultural preservation, and community welfare.

His vision for the Foundation reflects a belief that traditional wisdom and modern education can complement and strengthen one another. Through regular spiritual discourses, the encouragement of religious and philosophical study, and the organization of satsang yatras, he seeks to foster an environment of continuous learning, spiritual reflection, and community participation.

Drawing upon his extensive professional experience, he also recognizes the importance of mentorship, practical education, and access to opportunities in shaping the next generation. The Foundation's initiatives reflect this broader vision of empowering individuals through both spiritual guidance and professional development, while strengthening families and fostering a connected, self-reliant community.

Our initiatives

KalyanaSakha

Meaningful Connections, Rooted in Trust

KalyanaSakha is a community-oriented matrimonial initiative that brings families together through a thoughtful and personalized approach to matchmaking. Moving beyond conventional matrimonial platforms, it emphasizes trust, family involvement, and meaningful introductions through approved profiles, personal referrals, and carefully organized matchmaking gatherings. By creating a welcoming environment where families can interact and explore compatible alliances, KalyanaSakha seeks to make the journey toward marriage more personal, respectful, and rooted in shared values.

UdyogaSakha

Connecting Skills, Opportunities, and Communities

UdyogaSakha is a Foundation-governed initiative designed to connect individuals, professionals, and organizations through a structured and trustworthy opportunity ecosystem. Going beyond conventional employment portals, it seeks to facilitate employment, professional services, traditional occupations, entrepreneurship, skill development, and community-based engagements. Built around transparency, verification, and responsible participation, UdyogaSakha aims to help individuals discover meaningful opportunities while enabling organizations and service seekers to connect with credible professionals. The initiative reflects the Foundation's belief that every form of honest work deserves dignity, recognition, and opportunity.
